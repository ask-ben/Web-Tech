Why Readable Design Matters

Text and Spacing

Good typography makes content easy to scan. A comfortable font size and generous line height reduce eye strain and help readers keep their place.

Line height of around 1.6 gives each line room to breathe, especially in longer paragraphs of body text.

Color and Contrast

Color should support the content, not fight it. Dark text on a light background gives strong contrast, and a small palette keeps a page feeling consistent.

  • Use dark text on light backgrounds
  • Limit the palette to a few colors
  • Make links look different from normal text
